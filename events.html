Events

The Big Ones.

Satyanarayan Pooja

The Mechanical Engineering Students’ Association (MESA) annually organizes the sacred Shri Satyanarayan Pooja to seek divine blessings for a successful academic year and a harmonious department.

Highlights:

  • Departmental Unity: Gathers the Principal, Director, faculty, and students across all years.
  • Cultural Tradition: Features holy rituals followed by Prasadam distribution to foster togetherness and discipline.
  • Positive Beginnings: Sets a spiritually uplifting and harmonious tone for all upcoming MESA initiatives.

MMC
(Maharashtra Motor SportsClub)

Our MESA Heads and Board of Directors recently represented our organization at Offroad Escape, an off-road adventure and competition event hosted by the Maharashtra MotorSports Club (MMC).

During the event, our team:

  • Supported & Volunteered: Showed up on the ground to assist and represent MESA.
  • Networked & Connected: Built strong relationships with 4WD enthusiasts and key contacts from across India.
  • Gained Knowledge: Learned firsthand about managing extreme off-road events, safety protocols, and sportsmanship.

We are proud of our leadership team for stepping up and expanding MESA’s network!

Banner Reveal

The Auto Expo kicks off each year with an exhilarating Poster and Sponsor Reveal, setting the stage for a celebration of automotive innovation. Hosted at the Main Gate and Quadrangle, the tradition brings the community together with high-energy stunt performances, roaring engines, and exclusive poster unveilings.

Driven by a dedicated team of volunteers who manage crowds and engage attendees, the reveal continuously builds excitement, strengthens sponsor relationships, and sets a vibrant tone for the annual automotive extravaganza.

AutoExpo

Every year, MESA hosts its flagship Auto Expo—a premier celebration of automotive innovation, culture, and engineering talent.

The annual event kicks off with a grand inauguration alongside esteemed guests, followed by a dramatic entrance of riders and custom vehicles set to synchronized light and sound.

Key highlights:

  • Diverse Exhibits: A wide display ranging from futuristic electric models and hybrid prototypes to high-performance tuned cars and vintage motorcycles.
  • Thrilling Stunt Shows: High-energy stunt performances showcasing the strength, agility, and modern capabilities of display vehicles.
  • Interactive Zones: Dedicated exhibit sections featuring VR activities, informative booths, food stalls, and retail hubs to keep attendees engaged.

Driven by strong student leadership, MESA’s Auto Expo consistently provides a dynamic platform for knowledge sharing, networking, and setting new benchmarks.

MESA Social Responsibility
('Gad Samvardhan')

Under its social initiatives, the MESA team, in collaboration with the Durgvedh Yuva Maratha Foundation, participated in a Fort Conservation drive at Sarasgad, Pali.

During this campaign, members contributed voluntary physical service (Shramdaan) toward cleaning the fort premises, preserving the site, and protecting our historical heritage.

Demonstrating a strong sense of social responsibility, the youth effectively spread the vital message of fort conservation.

CAD WAR

  • Enhanced time management in competitive environments
