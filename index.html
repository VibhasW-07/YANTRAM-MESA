Introduction

At our college, the Mechanical Engineering Student Association (MESA) is a vibrant student group that acts as a hub for students with an interest in mechanical engineering. MESA provides mechanical engineering students chances for both professional and personal growth, and it is essential in creating a feeling of community among them.

One of MESA's primary objectives is to enhance the overall learning experience for its members. To achieve this, the association organizes a variety of events and workshops throughout the academic year. These activities cover a broad spectrum, including technical workshops, cultural events, sports activities, and more. MESA strives to create a holistic learning environment for students, nurturing both their technical skills and their personal interests.

Who are We?

Mechanical Engineering Students Association (MESA) of Bharati Vidyapeeth College of Engineering, Navi Mumbai organizes one of the Biggest AutoExpo event in Mumbai and MMR.

AutoExpo is an annual event where automobile enthusiasts from all over Maharashtra gather in our college to showcase their Highly Performanced and visual tuned cars, Super-bikes above 600cc, Modified and Customized Prototypes.

In AutoExpo riders show-off their riding and driving skills by doing stunts like Powerdrifting, Wheelie, Stoppie, Burnouts, etc.

Every year More than 400 bikers and 80+ cars participate in our event. Every year we successfully bring-in a footfall of 3000+ of college students and every year the crowd keep on increasing.
